Styling pages cover the values you give to modifiers such as `Background`, `Border` and `Shadow`. They are not
views of their own; the demos show them on boxes and other views.

- [Colors](colors/): literal and semantic colors, `Fade` and the theme roles.
- [Shadow and Border](shadow-and-border/): borders, shadows, corner radius and clipping.
- [Gradient](gradient/): a vertical background from one color to another.
- [Glass](glass/): a background material that tints and blurs what lies behind a view.
