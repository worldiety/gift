---
title: Dialogs & Overlays
weight: 5
---

Dialogs and overlays present a view on top of the screen and take its input while they are up.

- [Modal](modal/): presents any view centered over a scrim.
- [Alert](alert/): a card with a title, a message and a row of choices, shown in a modal.
- [Sheet](sheet/): a panel that slides up from the bottom edge.
- [Keyboard](keyboard/): the on-screen keyboard for touch kiosks without a hardware keyboard.
- [File Picker](file-picker/): opens files, chooses a folder or picks a path to save to, inside your window.
